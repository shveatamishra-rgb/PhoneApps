# Bhakti Angan: Launch-to-Diwali Marketing Calendar (Jul-Nov 2026)

One rule drives everything: every push is anchored to a festival the audience already cares about,
and every asset comes from the art we already own (61 remote images, gallery, widgets).
No spending until organic data says where to spend.

## The weekly engine (runs every week, ~2-3 hrs)

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Mon | 1 Reel + Short: darshan art zoom with mantra audio (India-first seeding: Hindi caption, IST posting, Indian hashtags from the researched list) |
| Wed | 1 Reel + Short: app feature clip (widget on home screen, japa counter, katha) |
| Fri | 1 Reel + Short: weekend darshan + "free wallpaper in bio" pointing to the gallery |
| Daily | WhatsApp Status image from the gallery (share button already on every card) |
| Sun | Check: installs, Pro conversions, gallery likes, Brevo opt-ins. 10 min, one sheet. |

Recycle every Reel as a YouTube Short and an Instagram Story. One asset, three surfaces.

## Phase 0: Submission week (Jul 7-12)

- [ ] Archive + upload v1.1 build (widgets included), submit for review with new screenshots (EN 5 panels, HI 5 panels, in AppStore/v1.1/)
- [ ] What's New copy in both languages (below)
- [ ] Announcement newsletter drafted in Brevo, held until approval
- [ ] On approval: release, then announcement Reel "Your home screen is now a mandir" (widget panel as the visual)

## Phase 1: Shravan (Jul 30 - Aug 27) "Shiva month"

Shravan Mondays: Aug 3, 10, 17, 24. This is the highest-intent Shiva audience of the year.

- Jul 30: Shravan kickoff. Newsletter: "Shravan begins today. A Shiva darshan every Somwar."
- Every Somwar (Mon): Shiva Reel at 6 AM IST (Adiyogi, Nataraja, Gangadhar, Neelkanth rotation), WhatsApp status, gallery Shiva collection link
- Aug 24 (last Somwar): "Shravan is ending" Reel + Pro pitch (the full Shiva collection lives in the app)
- App: no new build needed; remote pipeline can time a bonus Shiva drop (availableFrom) for Aug 3

## Phase 2: Janmashtami (Sep 3) "Krishna week"

- Aug 31 - Sep 2: teaser Reels from the 11-scene Krishna collection (Makhan Chor, Kaliya Mardan, Govardhan)
- Sep 3: midnight-janma Reel (Baby Krishna on the Yamuna), newsletter, WhatsApp burst
- Sep 4-6: "Which Krishna leela is your favourite?" engagement post; gallery Krishna collection link

## Phase 3: Ganesh Chaturthi (Sep 14) "Ganesha week"

- Sep 11-13: teasers (Bal Ganesha, Nritya Ganapati); the ganesha-chaturthi image is the hero
- Sep 14: festival Reel + newsletter; push the Daily Darshan widget angle (Ganesha on your home screen for the 10 days of the festival)
- Sep 23 (Visarjan): farewell post, "he returns next year, but the darshan stays in your pocket"

## Phase 4: Navratri (Oct 11-20) "Devi ninth-days" - biggest push of the season

The Devi set is complete: Sherawali, Navadurga, Vaishno Devi, Mahishasura Mardini, Parvati,
Lakshmi, Saraswati, Kali, Devi Maa. Nine days, nine posts, zero new art needed.

- Oct 8-10: "Navratri is coming" teasers + newsletter announcing a darshan for each of the nine nights
- Oct 11-19: one Devi Reel + WhatsApp status per day, 6 AM IST. Day 1 Shailputri energy = Navadurga image crops, then rotate the set
- Oct 13 (approx Saraswati/durga ashtami window varies): Saraswati and Lakshmi posts mid-festival
- Oct 20 (Vijayadashami): Mahishasura Mardini Reel, "good wins" + Pro pitch (complete Devi collection, App Store review-safe wording)
- Paid test window: if organic data by Oct 1 shows conversion, run Apple Search Ads Basic at a small daily cap on brand-adjacent terms ("durga darshan app", "panchang hindi") for the 10 days. Kill if install cost exceeds a week of organic.

## Phase 5: Diwali (Nov 8) "Lakshmi + lights"

- Nov 1-7: countdown Reels (Ayodhya Deepotsav image, Lakshmi Gajalakshmi image), newsletter "Diwali muhurat in your Choghadiya widget"
- Nov 8: Diwali greeting asset EVERYONE can forward (gallery Lakshmi with app link), peak WhatsApp forwarding day of the year
- Nov 9-14: Bhai Dooj / Chhath tail posts, then year-end review of what worked

## Standing rules

- India-first seeding on every Short/Reel (Hindi caption, IST posting, Indian hashtag set), per the audience diagnosis
- Every image passes the iconography gate before it ships anywhere
- No em dashes in any copy
- No priced screenshots, no third-party platform names in ASC metadata
- Metrics that matter, in order: installs, day-7 retention, Pro trial starts, gallery opt-ins. Likes are vanity.
