# Marketing execution pack — launch to Diwali 2026

Companion to `MARKETING_CALENDAR_2026.md` (the WHEN). This is the WHAT: every
reel script, image prompt, Grok image-to-video prompt, caption, newsletter, and
exact IST posting time, ready to produce and schedule.

## House production recipe (same for every reel)

1. **Stills**: generate 2-4 images per reel with the prompts below (9:16,
   1080x1920). Keep the house style suffix on every prompt:
   `ultra-detailed devotional oil painting, warm temple golds and deep reds,
   soft rim light, divine glow, intricate jewelry and fabric, serene expression,
   photorealistic painterly finish, vertical 9:16 composition`
2. **Motion**: animate each still in Grok Imagine (image-to-video) with the
   motion prompt given per asset. Keep clips 3-5s. General transition recipe:
   end clip A on a slow push-in, start clip B on a slow pull-back, cut on the
   beat; or use a 0.3s golden-glow crossfade (diya flame flash) between scenes.
3. **Assembly**: CapCut. Devotional track (own/licensed only, or IG's library
   inside the app so rights are covered). Text overlays: EN headline top, HI
   below in Devanagari (use ITF Devanagari, never auto-font). App Store panel
   or phone mockup as the last shot + end card.
4. **End card (every reel)**: app icon + "Bhakti Angan · Daily Darshan" +
   "Free on the App Store" + QR (optional for feed, skip on reels).
5. Export 1080x1920, schedule in Meta Business Suite (IG+FB together) and
   YouTube Studio (Shorts). Post natively, never watermark-crossposted.

**Standing IST time slots** (devotional audience peaks; from our YouTube
diagnosis: seed Indian viewers first):
- Morning puja slot: **6:00-6:30 AM IST** (Somwar/Shiva, Navratri daily)
- General reels: **7:30-8:00 PM IST** (evening aarti scroll)

- YouTube Shorts: 6:00 AM IST upload (India audience seeding)

Hashtag base (append the researched set from the 60-day plan xlsx, column K):

given per asset. 8-12 total, never more.
